How It Works

Your Mount Rushmore is the four directors, writers, and actors who define your personal taste — scored by how much of their work you've seen and how highly you rated it, how far above the audience and above your own average you rate their films, and how good their best work is to you.
The Scoring Formula
📈 Volume × Quality
Films seen × avg rating on a log scale — 10 films at 4★ beat 3 at 5★, with diminishing returns. Volume stops counting past 16 films: more of someone is not more love
50%
✨ Vs the Crowd
How far above the audience average you rate their films — the people who are yours rather than everyone's. A full star above the crowd fills the bar
20%
💛 Vs Your Average
How far above your own average you rate them, so a generous rater and a harsh one are read on their own scale. Both comparisons are discounted while the sample is small — 3 rated films count half
15%
🏔 Peak
The average of your three highest ratings for them — two of your favourite films ever count for more than a steady run of 4★
10%
🔁 Rewatches
Films you've logged more than once — a strong signal of genuine love
5%
Weights are normalized so the relative ratios always matter, even if they don't sum to 100%. Adjust them with the ⚖ Settings button. Minimum 3 rated films required to qualify (in a library with no ratings, every watched film counts).
Your Data
Your Letterboxd export is processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server except the TMDB API for director/writer/actor and streaming lookups. Your API key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device. Your last analysis is remembered in this browser too, so a refresh doesn't mean re-uploading — use Forget on the upload page to remove it along with the API key, fetched filmographies and streaming lookups, which is worth doing on a shared computer.
Filmography Coverage
Each director card shows how much of their feature filmography you've seen. What counts as a feature is two tests, both adjustable under ⚖ Settings: a length test (40 minutes by default, the Academy's threshold) and a kind test — documentaries and concert films are excluded by default, since for most directors they're a separate body of work. Turn Count documentaries on for someone like Herzog or Morris, where the docs are the filmography. The ≈ figure is measured against the built-in database, which holds their best-known work rather than every title, so it reads high for directors with obscure or early films. Click the bar to see exactly which films you're missing, listed by year. Hit ⟳ Exact filmography there (or on the directors panel, for the top four) to pull the complete credits from TMDB — that needs a free API key, and the result is cached for next time. Coverage feeds the score in exactly one way: once you've seen every feature on the exact TMDB list, the director earns a small 🏁 100% bonus (+3 points by default, adjustable or switchable off under ⚖ Settings). The ≈ estimate shows the badge but never the points — "every film the database knows" is too often three of thirty. It's flat rather than proportional, so it settles close calls without vaulting someone past a director you clearly rate higher.
🍿 Watch Next
Every film you haven't seen by a director who qualifies — plus, if you leave it switched on, films starring the actors and written by the writers you rate highest — ranked on three axes: taste match (how its director, writers, cast and genres have landed with you), completion (how near you are to finishing them — both how far along you are and how few are left), and acclaim (the film's TMDB popularity and rating). The four modes are just different weightings of those same three, so each one still mixes directors you love with filmographies you're close to closing out. Nothing here is scored on films you haven't watched, so the wider your filmography data, the better it gets.
Switch to 📋 My watchlist and upload watchlist.csv from your export (or any Letterboxd list) to rank the films you've already picked out. Each is scored on how its director, writers, top-billed actors and genres have landed with you — how far above your own average you rate them, shrunk toward neutral when you've only seen one or two, with a little credit for how often you seek them out — plus its TMDB rating. The sliders set how much each one counts; the length chips and 🎲 Surprise me (a random pick from your ten best matches) help when it's time to choose. With a TMDB key each card shows where it's streaming in your country, your picked 📺 services are highlighted, and On my services narrows the watchlist to what you can put on now. Streaming data comes from JustWatch, via TMDB, and is cached in this browser for a few days. Where to watch links open JustWatch itself.
Writers & Actors
Writers are credited for Screenplay, Story, and Adaptation roles. Actors are the top 5 billed cast members per film. Both use the same scoring formula as directors.
